Meet the creator

Made to explore

Shamus Creates is the independent music project of James Drum, a songwriter and AI music creator based in the Detroit area. His work brings songwriting and artificial intelligence together to develop full songs, from concepts and lyrics to production-ready compositions, across a wide range of genres.

With a focus on writing lyrics, AI prompting, and exploring sounds beyond a single musical lane, Shamus Creates is built around one idea: follow the song wherever it leads. From the first concept to the final playback, each track is an opportunity to create something distinctive.
